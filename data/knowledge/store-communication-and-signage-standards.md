# Store Communication And Signage Standards

## Purpose
This Vivalis Retail Group document governs store communication and signage. It is grounded in incident INC-2026-0042: national shell-egg supply is short by 38% and consumer demand is up 44%. It references SKU-EGG-0001, SKU-EGG-0002, SKU-EGG-0003, SKU-EGG-0005, SKU-EGG-0008, liquid egg SKU-EGG-0009, plant-based SKU-ALT-0001 and dependent SKUs SKU-BAK-0101 and SKU-RDY-0201. Regions are NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST and CENTRE, served by DC-NORTH-01, DC-SOUTH-01, DC-EAST-01, DC-WEST-01 and DC-CENTRE-01.

## Thresholds
Act when any warehouse falls below 3 days of cover, any store cluster shelf availability is below 85%, or supplier confirmations fall below 75% of ordered cases. Southern clusters sit near 60-66% availability and traffic-driver warehouse cover is around 1.8-6.1 days. Promotions PRO-0001, PRO-0003 and PRO-0007 each create more than 30% uplift and should be cancelled. A two-pack customer limit starts below 85% availability; one-pack limits below 70% require executive approval.

## Procedure
The control tower meets at 07:30 and 16:00. Confirm product, supplier and DC ids; remove artificial demand; protect COM-0001, COM-0002, COM-0003, COM-0006 and COM-0011; allocate by four-week baseline demand; use alternates SUP-EGG-006, SUP-EGG-009 and SUP-EGG-010 before provisional suppliers; and record cases, EUR cost, days of cover before and after, and named approval. DC-CENTRE-01 is the balancing hub when donor cover remains above 4 days. Imported supply costs 20-45% more and adds 6-11 days lead time.

## Roles
STK-001 Hélène Marchand approves allocation. STK-002 Marc Bellier pauses promotions. STK-003 Sofia Laurent executes store limits. STK-004 Jonas Weber approves cost exposure above EUR 100,000. STK-005 Amina Roussel releases suppliers and recipe changes. STK-006 Claire Devaux approves customer wording. STK-007 Nicolas Armand reviews compliance. STK-008 Elise Fournier approves national limits or options above EUR 250,000.

## Escalation and evidence
Escalate if a chilled vehicle exceeds 8 C for more than 30 minutes, if a veterinary release is missing, if a label change is required, or if a recommendation changes an organic or free-range welfare promise. Evidence must include incident id, SKU id, supplier id, warehouse id, affected region, current service level, expected shelf recovery and the approving stakeholder. Customer language must say inspected eggs remain safe and limits share reduced supply fairly.
